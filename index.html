How Elsewhere works

Type what you do or say in your own words. Explore the station, square, Lantern Bar, library and cliff cottage. Speak to Rowan, Mira or the conductor. Inspect objects, ask questions, take or use items, or just rest.

The town has a persistent clock, characters remember some of your behavior, and unexpected events can interrupt you. Your choices have consequences. There is a solvable secret and more than one ending, but no countdown to failure.

Try: “I go to the library,” “I ask Mira about Evelyn,” “I inspect the clock,” “I take the gear,” “I rest,” or “I try to force the door.” More imaginative actions are welcome, though this offline prototype understands a limited set of intents.

This version runs entirely in your browser, without an AI model. It interprets many natural-language actions using local rules; it does not genuinely understand arbitrary text. Future online AI support will need a secure backend. Progress is saved on this device.

Player notes

No points, daily streaks, or affection meters. You can leave the mystery alone. The world will still notice you.
